METHODOLOGY

Data-driven design, not vibes.

Every threshold below is a constant in the code — not a subjective call made per invoice. This is what actually decides tone, risk, and priority.

Urgency thresholds Days overdue → tone

Tone escalates at fixed cut-points — 5, 13, and 25 days — not a subjective judgment call made per invoice.

Client risk cutoffs Avg. days late → risk band

A client averaging just 3 days late already tips into "Medium" — the bands are intentionally tight.

Priority score weighting Max points contributed, of 10

Urgency carries the most weight (4 of 10) — a large invoice can't out-rank a severely overdue one just by size.
